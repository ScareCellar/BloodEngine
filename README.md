Welcome to Blood!

This is a twin-stick shooter featuring multiple weapon types and increasing difficulty over time.

Core Features include:
  Bullets,
  Rockets,
  Nukes,
  Enemies,
  Basic Collision,
  Score System,
  Most other systems we covered in this class.

Additional features:
  increasing difficulty,
  multiple weapon types,
  weapon reload mechanic,
  self-damaging nuke.

To play the game, simply download the zip and open the Game.exe file.

Controls:
  A and D to pivot,
  W and S to move forward and backward,
  Left Click to shoot bullets,
  Right Click to shoot piercing rockets,
  Middle Mouse Click to launch a nuke.

  SPACE to start the game,
  R to restart on game over screen.
